
Thinking through the idea of a Seseragi color scheme and how to define variables for colors.

I suppose it depends on the perspective on colors 

If the color scheme for Seseragi defines white as off-white then describing it as "Seseragi white" makes sense.

Or if there might be actual white one could define

--white: #fff
--seseragi-white: #eee

But that implies that white is outside the color scheme yet its being used in the actual design as a color.

An alternative is defining white as pure white and off white as off-white

--white: #fff
--off-white: #eee

The original reasoning was that while working on the Seseragi design that colors that were known to be in the Seseragi color scheme would be prefixed "--seseragi" leading to:

--white: #fff
--seseragi-white: #eee

but there is also background color for the design

--bg-color: #eee

The choice is between setting the standard white color to the Seseragi color (--color-white: #eee) or specifying a color scheme color (--seseragi-white: #eee).
